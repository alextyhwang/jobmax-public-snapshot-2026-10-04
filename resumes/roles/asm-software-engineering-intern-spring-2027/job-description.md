Twelve-week, full-time on-site software engineering internship in Phoenix beginning January/February 2027 and ending April/May. Key signals: computer science or related degree; software development, systems programming, or backend experience; automation; validation; developer and QA tooling; configuration and logs; reliability; collaboration in semiconductor engineering.
