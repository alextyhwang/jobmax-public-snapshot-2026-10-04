## Division and Program

This opportunity resides with Warfare Systems, a business group within HII's Mission Technologies division. The division supports national-security customers across C5ISR, AI and Big Data, cyber operations, synthetic training environments, fleet sustainment, environmental remediation, and unmanned underwater vehicles.

The 2027 Software Engineering Internship is based at HII Mission Technologies' Columbia, Maryland location. Interns support **Minotaur**, a mission-management system used across manned and unmanned surveillance aircraft. The work improves how operators detect, track, and understand activity on land and at sea by enhancing sensor-data-processing capabilities.

## What You'll Do

- Build and refine new features for operational software.
- Contribute to all phases of the software development lifecycle: design, implementation, integration, and testing.
- Collaborate closely with engineers and other interns on meaningful, real-world projects.

Potential project areas include:

- Developing solutions to high-priority operational challenges using machine learning, AI, signal processing, and video analytics.
- Designing intuitive, user-focused graphical interfaces.
- Improving and automating simulation, test, and verification workflows.
- Creating proof-of-concept prototypes for emerging technologies and future capabilities.

## What HII Is Looking For

Candidates must be currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field. The role is designed for rising college juniors.

Intern levels are determined by completed degree credits:

- **Level 4:** More than 60% of required bachelor's-degree credits completed.
- **Level 3:** More than 40% through 60% of required credits completed.
- **Level 2:** More than 20% through 40% of required credits completed.

HII seeks students with:

- Experience with software development in a Linux environment.
- Proficiency in languages such as C++, Java, or JavaScript.
- Strong communication skills and critical-thinking abilities.
- A proactive, hardworking mindset and a strong desire to learn.
