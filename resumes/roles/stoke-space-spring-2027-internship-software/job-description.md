## Full Job Description

Stoke Space is hiring Spring 2027 software interns in Kent, WA. The role is onsite and requires full-time availability for 10-12 consecutive weeks. Stoke describes interns as working on real, open-ended problems with mentorship from experienced engineers and direct ownership of high-impact projects.

General qualifications:

- Pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in CS, computer engineering, engineering, or a related STEM field.
- Project-level experience with Python, C, or Rust.
- Strong engineering fundamentals.
- Hands-on engineering experience through internships, projects, work, or teams.
- Ability to manage a complex technical project through execution.
- Ability to learn and apply state-of-the-art techniques.
- Strong written and verbal communication.

Software teams:

- Boltline: web-based SaaS for advanced hardware manufacturing teams. Full lifecycle work across design, implementation, testing, and deployment. Requires JavaScript or TypeScript and front-end or back-end experience. Preferred stack includes TypeScript, React, Next.js, Apollo, Express.js, Node.js, GraphQL, PostgreSQL, and AWS.
- Vehicle Software: control software for testers, engine test stands, and flight systems. Requires Rust or systems languages such as C/C++ and real-time systems experience.
- Data: real-time and post-launch data converted into analysis and insights. Requires Rust and front-end or back-end experience; preferred AWS, time series data, Svelte, WebGPU.

Compensation listed by Stoke: freshman/sophomore $28/hour, junior/senior $33/hour, completed bachelor's $35/hour, completed master's $40/hour.
