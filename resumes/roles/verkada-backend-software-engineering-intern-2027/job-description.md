# Verkada — Backend Software Engineering Intern 2027

- Work model: On-site at Verkada HQ

## Who We Are

Verkada builds an integrated, privacy-sensitive, AI-powered physical-security platform spanning video security, access control, air-quality sensors, alarms, intercoms, and visitor management. The posting describes more than 30,000 customers, over 2 million deployed devices across 170+ countries, and an emphasis on physical AI.

## About the Role

Verkada is seeking software engineering interns who can thrive in a startup culture and launch products used by customers globally. The intern joins the Backend Engineering team, works across the full software stack, and collaborates cross-functionally on software supporting Verkada's security systems. The role requires on-site work at Verkada HQ in San Mateo.

## What You'll Do

- Build scalable distributed systems capable of handling high traffic from hundreds of thousands of devices worldwide.
- Work with high-concurrency key-value storage systems such as Redis and DynamoDB.
- Work with Postgres relational databases.
- Work with logging and message-passing technologies such as Kafka and SQS.
- Deploy services through Terraform and Kubernetes.
- Develop large-scale systems that interact with and configure products remotely.
- Define and improve low-latency, high-throughput, high-reliability microservice architectures.
- Design and develop features incorporating computer vision.
- Design clean APIs and implement them with Python and/or Go.

## What You Bring

- Ability to work at Verkada HQ during winter or summer 2027.
- Active pursuit of a bachelor's or master's degree in Computer Science or a similar technical field, with graduation by June 2028.
- Prior internship experience developing and launching products.
- Familiarity with Python or Golang, distributed systems, operating systems, AWS, Docker, and related technologies.
- Comfort working in an agile team software-development environment.
- Familiarity with REST APIs and Linux servers is a plus.
