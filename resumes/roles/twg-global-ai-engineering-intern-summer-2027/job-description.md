## Full Job Description

 The Organization   At TWG Group Holdings, LLC (“TWG Global”), we drive innovation and business transformation across a diverse portfolio—including investments, finance, insurance, and media—by leveraging data and AI as core assets. Our AI-first, cloud-native approach delivers real-time intelligence and interactive business applications, empowering informed decision-making for both customers and employees.  We prioritize responsible data and AI practices, ensuring ethical standards and regulatory compliance. Our decentralized structure enables each business unit to operate autonomously, supported by the central AI Solutions Group, while strategic partnerships with leading data and AI vendors fuel transformative projects in marketing, operations, and product development.  You will collaborate with management to advance our data and analytics transformation, enhance productivity, and enable agile, data-driven decisions. By leveraging relationships with top tech startups and universities, you will help create competitive advantages and drive industry innovation.  At TWG Global, your contributions will support our goal of sustained growth and superior returns, as we deliver significant value and impact across our businesses.  The Role    Step into an exhilarating role as an AI Engineering Intern on our AI Engineering Team , where you'll architect and deploy groundbreaking machine learning (ML) and AI solutions that propel our organization forward. You'll be the driving force behind scalable ML models and cloud-based AI platforms, optimized for high-performance data pipelines that enable lightning-fast training and inference. But that's just the start, you'll pioneer agentic AI systems, orchestrating multi-agent workflows that leverage sub-agents for tasks like semantic search, data retrieval, and automated analysis, all integrated with ontology-driven knowledge bases for intelligent, persistent insights.  Collaborate with visionary research, product, and cross-functional teams to infuse the latest ML and generative AI advancements - like large language models (LLMs), and agentic platforms, into our products and services. You'll craft personalized AI experiences, evolve reference architectures for cloud-native AI/ML, and ensure they embody cutting-edge standards. Plus, you’ll champion the adoption of these technologies organization-wide, empowering leaders and teams to slash costs, spark new revenue, and dominate markets - all while upholding top-tier quality, security, and compliance.  Your mastery of cloud engineering, ML optimization, and collaborative innovation will unlock high-impact AI that cements our leadership. By weaving in agentic elements like sub-task orchestration, Knowledge Nodes for capturing and reusing insights, and artifact management for seamless workflows, you'll help deliver transformative products that redefine what's possible and secure our competitive edge.  Key Responsibilities:  Assist in building and deploying agentic frameworks and applications, RAG pipelines and retrieval architectures, and automated workflows for structured data analysis; contribute to applied work in small language model tuning and computer vision as needed. Collaborate with research, engineering, and product teams to help integrate new ML innovations into feed products and services, contributing to improved user experiences. Support the implementation of advanced personalization features and exploration capabilities, helping to drive improvements in product metrics and user engagement. Contributes to the design and development of scalable frontend and backend solutions, following best practices in technical design, test automation, and code quality. Help optimize models, data pipelines, and retrieval systems for robust, efficient, and reliable operation in production. Build and iterate on applications and data workflows using the Palantir platform (Foundry/AIP), including ontology modeling and AIP-driven agentic workflows. Work with cross-functional teams—including program managers, data engineers, data scientists, and business analysts—to support the integration and execution of AI/ML solutions and assist in resolving system issues. Participate in cloud engineering projects to help enable scalable ML platforms for training and deploying cloud-based AI/ML solutions, focusing on performance and security. Support the integration and deployment of generative AI solutions using cloud-based models, contributing to innovation in new and existing products. Assist in delivering scalable ML solutions, helping to identify and validate deployment configurations for optimal performance and resource use. Contribute to the design and maintenance of reference architectures and frameworks that support diverse AI/ML workloads. Support the development and deployment of personalized large language models (LLMs) to enhance search and recommendation systems and participate in research on emerging LLM techniques. Support the development, evaluation, and deployment of LLM-based applications, with exposure to model customization and emerging techniques as projects require. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to help integrate LLMs into products and services, ensuring new technologies are effectively leveraged. Assist in executing roadmaps for search and personalization initiatives and help build partnerships with internal and external stakeholders to drive innovation in AI/ML projects.
