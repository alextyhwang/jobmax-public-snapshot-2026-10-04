Source: https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/etched/27e5bd6b-9357-45f0-9e79-cfa2bf4eeba8

Checked: 2026-08-20

Location: San Jose, CA (on-site)

Terms: Fall 2026, Spring 2027, and Summer 2027; 12-week paid internship.

## Role

Co-design, develop, and test simulations of Etched's custom hardware and the tools engineers use to analyze those systems. Work closely with software and hardware teams across the development path.

## Qualifications

- Progress toward a bachelor's, master's, or PhD in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field.
- Proficiency in C/C++ or Rust.
- Strong data structures and algorithms fundamentals.
- Strong understanding of low-level software engineering.
- Strong understanding of hardware/software co-design.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills.

## Nice To Have

- Linux internals, kernel development, or driver debugging.
- Hardware diagnostics or hardware-log interpretation.
- Server virtualization or CI/CD pipelines.
- Rust or embedded-development experience.

## Program Details

- Housing support for interns who relocate.
- Daily lunch and dinner in the office.
- Direct mentorship from industry engineers.
- Fully in-person at Etched's San Jose office.

Etched explicitly encourages candidates to apply even if they do not meet every qualification.
