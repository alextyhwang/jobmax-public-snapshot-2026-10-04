## Full Job Description

Software Engineer Intern Fall 2026/Winter 2027

Skydio
Location: US CA San Mateo
Employment Type: Intern
Department: R&D

Skydio is the leading US drone company and the world leader in autonomous flight, combining deep expertise in artificial intelligence, hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession.

About the role:

Skydio is looking for an intern to join in Fall 2026 or Winter 2027. As an intern on the Cloud Platform and Infrastructure team, the intern will help build and scale the cloud systems behind drones used for real-world inspection, public safety, and defense use cases.

Possible work includes developing and packaging a small form factor Skydio cloud offering for airgapped or edge environments.

What makes you a good fit:

- Solid computer science fundamentals.
- Meaningful project and/or internship experience developing complex web applications or backend data systems.
- Proficiency with Python, Golang, and/or JavaScript/TypeScript.
- Familiarity with RESTful APIs, Linux operating system configuration, build tools, relational databases such as Postgres/MySQL, and caching systems such as Redis/Memcached.
- Understanding of cloud service offerings such as AWS, GCP, Azure, deployment techniques, and configuration management.

Compensation:

- $47/hr for undergraduate students.
- $53/hr for graduate students.
- $58/hr for PhD students.
