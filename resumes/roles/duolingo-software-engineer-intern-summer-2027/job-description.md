## Full Job Description

Duolingo's mission is to develop the best education in the world and make it
universally available. The company emphasizes learner impact, experimentation,
fact-based decisions, and innovative solutions to complex technical problems.

### What you'll do

- Contribute to full-stack software projects by developing, testing, and
  maintaining features and services.
- Develop, release, and maintain services and/or infrastructure.
- Lead individual project priorities, deadlines, and deliverables.
- Contribute to code reviews and collaborate across teams to ensure quality and
  scalability.

### What we're looking for

- Graduation between December 2027 and August 2028.
- Pursuing a B.S. or M.S. in Computer Science or a related technical field.
- Dedication to Duolingo's mission of universally available education.
- Programming experience in Java, Python, Kotlin, or Swift.
- Twelve consecutive weeks of availability during May 24-August 13, 2027 or
  June 14-September 3, 2027.
- Application deadline: September 30, 2026.

### Examples of past intern projects

- GPT-4 chat interface for an internal content-creation tool.
- A/B-testing and observability integration to flag experiment errors.
- Spanish audio lessons and Math-course coordinate lessons.
- Porting large features across iOS, Android, and web.
- Site-reliability dashboards and tooling.
- New microservices built from scratch.

Hourly range: $54-$56 USD. Locations listed: Seattle, Pittsburgh, and New York.
