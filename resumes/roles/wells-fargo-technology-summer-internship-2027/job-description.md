## Program and responsibilities

Wells Fargo is seeking aspiring Software Engineers for the 2027 Technology Internship Program. Participants work on impactful, production-aligned projects, gain exposure to modern engineering practices and AI-enabled development workflows, and participate in training, mentorship, and networking with senior technology leaders. Successful interns may be considered for the following year's Technology Analyst Program in the same market.

- Design, develop, test, and deploy scalable applications, including enhancements, bug fixes, and new services across enterprise platforms.
- Collaborate in Agile Scrum/Kanban teams to deliver high-quality, secure, maintainable code.
- Leverage enterprise-approved AI tools such as Copilot and Devin to generate unit and integration tests aligned with QA standards; assist code development, documentation, and refactoring; and support rapid prototyping and solution exploration in sandbox/non-production environments.
- Partner with senior engineers to review, validate, and govern AI-generated code for enterprise architecture, risk, and security standards.
- Contribute to automated documentation, code review assistance, and developer productivity tooling within approved SDLC processes.
- Explore AI/ML application capabilities such as intelligent automation, predictive insights, and natural-language interfaces aligned with Model Risk Management and regulatory requirements.
- Apply prompt engineering to improve accuracy, efficiency, and reliability while adhering to data-handling and confidentiality policies.
- Build and maintain APIs, microservices, and cloud applications.
- Contribute to data modeling, pipelines, and analytics environments.
- Support reusable frameworks and modern design patterns across distributed systems.
- Partner with architecture, product, and platform teams to evolve services.

## Qualifications

Required: 6+ months of work experience or equivalent through work, training, military experience, or education.

Desired:

- Pursuing a bachelor's degree in CS, Computer Engineering, or related STEM field; expected graduation December 2027–June 2028.
- Foundational knowledge of Java, Python, .NET, SQL, or cloud technologies.
- Understanding of algorithms, data structures, and API development.
- SDLC experience including CI/CD, testing, deployment.
- Problem solving and interpersonal skills; articulate challenges and recommend solutions.
- Exposure to AI or AI-assisted tools such as GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, and code assistants.
- Familiarity with prompt engineering and responsible AI usage; interest in AI's impact on engineering workflows and productivity.
- Prior engineering internship experience.
- Initiative, urgency, curiosity, learning, and growth.
- Extracurricular enrichment such as volunteerism, student organizations, study abroad, leadership, or nonprofit involvement.
