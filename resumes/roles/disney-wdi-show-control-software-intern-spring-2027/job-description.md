## Full Job Description

Walt Disney Imagineering's Show Software Engineering team develops software for attractions, guest-interactive systems, and park/resort-wide electronic systems. The approximate internship dates are January through June 2027, with possible placement in Glendale, California or the Orlando/Lake Buena Vista, Florida area.

### Responsibilities

- Develop software for guest-interactive systems, leading small projects or contributing to larger software teams.
- Develop and review requirements, specifications, system architectures, module-level designs, data-flow diagrams, software, and test plans.
- Participate in software and system testing; write maintainable engineering and user documentation; release software.
- Evaluate technologies and methods that could reduce development or maintenance cost.
- Contribute to personal schedule and effort estimates.
- Help define interfaces between controls software and show mechanical/audio elements.
- Develop and review scopes of work and Test and Adjust plans and procedures.
- Verify software performance against requirements and review acceptance-testing deliverables.
- Support operator and maintenance training, present project updates, and collaborate with engineers and external organizations.

### Required Qualifications

- Scientific or real-time programming experience with technologies such as PLCs, C/C++/Visual C, C#, Python, JavaScript, or Ruby.
- Experience with technologies such as .NET, PowerShell, Unity/Unreal, Chef, MATLAB, Windows, or software deployment.
- Problem analysis and resolution skills and an understanding of software-engineering principles, control systems, and software systems.
- Valid driver's license.

### Preferred Qualifications

- At least one year of relevant work or internship experience.
- Experience with human-interface devices such as touchscreens, Kinect, or real-world sensing.
- Broadcast engineering or film-industry tools development.
- Knowledge of WDI or industry standards and an understanding of circuits, relays, and serial communication.

### Education and Program Information

- Currently enrolled as a junior or higher in an accredited college or university, pursuing engineering, science, computer science, or a related degree.
- Must meet Disney program eligibility requirements, be fully available for the January-June 2027 term, and provide reliable transportation.
- California interns provide their own Glendale-area housing; limited Florida housing may be available.
- Posted undergraduate pay rate: $43 per hour.
