## Job Description

Thomson Reuters Labs is seeking a curious and open-minded ML/NLP scientist intern with an interest in programming, machine learning, and solving problems using state-of-the-art Machine Learning and Natural Language Processing techniques.

The Labs experiments, builds, and delivers applied research and new products and technologies across Legal, Tax & Accounting, Government, and Reuters News. The Applied Scientist Intern joins a diverse, interdisciplinary global team with specialists in Information Retrieval, Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, Engineering, and Design. The team delivers data-driven Artificial Intelligence solutions for Thomson Reuters products and customers.

## Key Responsibilities

- **Experiment and Develop:** Participate in the entire model-development lifecycle, building, testing, and delivering high-quality solutions.
- **Collaborate:** Work on a collaborative, cross-functional team and learn from and partner with colleagues across the globe.
- **Innovate:** Try new approaches and technologies, contribute ideas, and solve real-world challenges.

## Required Qualifications

The posting says a candidate's background should include some of the following:

- Currently enrolled in a PhD program in a relevant discipline, or in a Master's program with research experience.
- Understanding of and experience with Natural Language Processing and/or Machine Learning methods, including Large Language Models.
- Experience with model training, fine-tuning, and evaluating experiments related to Machine Learning or Large Language Models.
- Experience in one or more programming languages, preferably Python, and experience working in the cloud, preferably AWS.
- Experience with ML/NLP libraries such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, scikit-learn, spaCy, LangChain, and Hugging Face.
- Comfort working with unstructured datasets and knowledge of data-cleaning and data-processing tools and techniques.
- Good communication skills and the ability to collaborate as part of a team.

## Applied Science Internship Opportunities

- On-the-job coaching and learning with current methods and technologies.
- Work with large datasets and high-impact products. Thomson Reuters says its scientists and engineers work with more than 60,000 TB of legal, regulatory, news, and tax data.
- Competitive compensation and skill development.
