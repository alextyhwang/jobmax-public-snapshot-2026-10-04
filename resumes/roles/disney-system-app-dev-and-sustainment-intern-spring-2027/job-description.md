## Full Job Description

About the Role & Program

The System App Dev & Sustainment Analyst Intern is a full-stack development role that works with leadership and technical teams. The intern partners across Disney Central, DRTSC, and Disney Global Business Technology to help ensure smooth system performance, conduct regular audits, deliver sustainable solutions on short timelines, and provide system administration and support.

What You Will Do

- Develop applications to support business processes.
- Troubleshoot applications after development and provide end-user support.
- Partner with other application owners to create and use APIs.
- Conduct project requirement-gathering sessions.
- Develop training programs for application rollouts.
- Manage and prioritize multiple tasks independently in a fast-paced environment.
- Work with cross-functional teams across business units with varying priorities.

Required Qualifications & Skills

- Proven experience with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Java, and SQL.
- Strong knowledge of REST, jQuery, AJAX, XML, JSON, React, Spring Boot, and Maven.
- Demonstrated proficiency with Git.
- Demonstrated proficiency with Linux and Bash, preferably Fedora-like systems such as CentOS or RHEL.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills.
- Ability to prioritize multiple assignments simultaneously.
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines such as GitLab CI.

Preferred Qualifications & Skills

- Previous project management experience.
- Experience with Docker/Kubernetes and TypeScript.
- Experience with the Mantine React.js design framework.
- Knowledge of OAuth2/OIDC.
- Experience with unit testing using Jest, JUnit, or similar tools.
- Experience with Babel, Webpack, or other web compiling and bundling technologies.
- Familiarity with Hibernate, JPA, or other ORMs.
- Working experience with ServiceNow.
- Call center experience.
- Basic familiarity with artificial intelligence concepts and emerging AI tools.

Education

Currently enrolled undergraduate student studying Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Data Engineering, Information Systems, Software Engineering, or a related field at an accredited college or university.

Eligibility Requirements & Program Information

- At the time of application, must be enrolled in an accredited college or university taking at least one class in the spring or fall term before participation, or currently participating in a Disney College Program or Disney Internship.
- Must be at least 18 years old.
- Must possess unrestricted work authorization.
- Must have a consistent, reliable schedule and be fully available Monday through Friday, Eastern Time, 40 hours per week.
- Must have reliable transportation and provide own housing in the Orlando area.
- Approximate internship dates are January 2027 through June 2027.

The pay rate for this role in Orlando, Florida is $43 per hour.
