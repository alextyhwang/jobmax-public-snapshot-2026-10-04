No Visa Sponsorship
Develop critical web applications for the Product Development Portal that streamline and accelerate Rivian's vehicle design and manufacturing lifecycles. Drive massive engineering efficiency and productivity gains by optimizing complex workflows, resulting in savings of a multitude of millions each year. Build operational resiliency through robust digital infrastructure that directly enhances product quality and ensures the delivery of superior electric vehicles.
Responsibilities
Execute full-stack development from day one, independently building, testing, and deploying frontend and backend features for Rivian's Product Development Portal.
Architect and implement scalable web applications that automate complex workflows, directly driving engineering productivity and multi-million dollar annual savings.
Strengthen system resiliency by writing clean, maintainable code that supports critical design and manufacturing lifecycles to enhance overall vehicle quality.
Collaborate cross-functionally with engineering and product teams to rapidly prototype and ship intuitive, user-centric internal tools in a fast-paced environment.
Take immediate technical ownership by proactively troubleshooting issues, participating in code reviews, and contributing to system architecture decisions to continuously improve platform performance.
Qualifications
Must be currently pursuing a bachelors or masters degree at an accredited university.
Actively pursuing a degree or one closely related in computer science or similar.
Prior software engineering internship or co-op experience, establishing the ability to instantly integrate into agile teams, independently navigate production codebases, and ship robust features from day one.
Strong foundation in scalable systems architecture, including hands-on experience with REST/GraphQL APIs, database design (SQL/NoSQL), and cloud infrastructure (such as AWS).
Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills, with a proven ability to translate complex engineering bottlenecks into intuitive, highly resilient digital tools that drive enterprise efficiency.
