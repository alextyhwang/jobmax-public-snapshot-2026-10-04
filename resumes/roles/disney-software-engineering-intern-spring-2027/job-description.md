## Full Job Description

About the Role & Program

Disney Experiences brings Disney stories and franchises to life through theme parks, resorts, cruise ships, vacation experiences, products, and more. Disney is seeking a curious, enthusiastic Software Engineering Intern who is eager to learn. This entry-level role supports the team in delivering experiences to guests and customers.

This is a full-time, six-month internship in the Orlando, Florida area from January 2027 through June 2027. The role primarily works from the Orlando Disney Central location and focuses on custom web-based applications that improve contact-center efficiency.

The position uses object-oriented languages such as C#, Java, and JavaScript, including Node and TypeScript. It also involves administering and building databases for custom applications using Postgres, Informix, Microsoft SQL, or other platforms. The intern will integrate vendor telephony and SaaS products across The Walt Disney Company, including incident-management platforms and Single Sign-On solutions, to provide secure, user-friendly applications used by many business segments.

The internship uses scripting languages for scheduled tasks and reporting jobs; enterprise security and design best practices; Jira, Zendesk, and ServiceNow for release timelines; Jenkins and GitHub Actions for continuous integration and delivery; and Git, GitHub, and GitLab for version control.

What You Will Do

- Write and maintain custom web and service-based applications in C#, Java, or JavaScript that integrate with SaaS and telephony products.
- Build efficient, secure, and user-friendly experiences for internal Disney teams.
- Administer, build, and maintain databases and server infrastructure for custom and vendor applications.
- Maintain well-documented code and version control with Git, GitHub, or GitLab so other Cast Members can support and continue the products.
- Write continuous-delivery workflows with Jenkins and GitHub Actions.
- Write Bash and Batch scripts for scheduled tasks and reporting jobs.
- Brainstorm projects from inception through delivery and demonstrate cost or user-experience benefits to key stakeholders.
- Track project and incident progress in task-management platforms and communicate due dates to leaders.

Required Qualifications & Skills

- Experience in one or more object-oriented programming languages such as C#, Java, or JavaScript.
- Willingness to learn new programming languages with team guidance.
- Proven experience with SQL platforms such as Informix, Postgres, or Microsoft SQL.
- Experience with version-control and CI/CD platforms such as Git, GitHub, Jenkins, or GitHub Actions.

Education

Currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.

Eligibility Requirements & Program Information

- Must be enrolled in an accredited college or university, take at least one class in the spring or fall term before participation, and return to school in the term after the internship, or currently participate in a Disney College Program or Disney Internship.
- Must be at least 18 years old.
- Must possess unrestricted work authorization.
- Must have a consistent, reliable schedule and be fully available Monday through Friday, Eastern Time, 40 hours per week.
- Must have reliable transportation and provide own housing in the Orlando area.

The pay rate for this role in Orlando, Florida is $43 per hour.
