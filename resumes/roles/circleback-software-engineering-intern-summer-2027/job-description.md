## Full Job Description

Circleback is hiring a Software Engineering Intern for Summer 2027 in San Francisco. The Y Combinator listing describes Circleback as an AI-powered meeting notes and automations company building a source of truth for company conversations across meetings, calls, emails, and related workflows.

Role details:

- Internship, full-stack engineering, San Francisco, CA.
- Compensation listed on YC: $7K-$10K/month.
- School year listed: junior and above.
- Visa: will sponsor.
- Skills listed: PostgreSQL, React, React Native, TypeScript.

Responsibilities:

- Build new features end-to-end across database models, API endpoints, and UI components for web, desktop, and mobile apps.
- Improve AI-powered outcomes, search, Circleback Assistant, transcription, on-device recording/streaming, app and API performance, automations, and new product initiatives.
- Turn customer conversations and learnings into product improvements.

Candidate signals:

- Previous role or side-project experience building full-stack software.
- High attention to detail, curiosity about great products, urgency, ownership, and pride in craft.
- Strong product sense is helpful but not strictly required.

Company signals:

- Profitable, growing virally, and used by paying customers.
- Founder/engineering backgrounds include Stripe, Twitter, Tableau, and Microsoft.

Compensation and logistics:
