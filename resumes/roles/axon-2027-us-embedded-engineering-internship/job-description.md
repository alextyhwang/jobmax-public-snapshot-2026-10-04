## Full Job Description

<p><strong>Your Impact</strong></p>
<p>As a Embedded Engineer Intern, you have foundational computer science knowledge and a strong command of one or more object-oriented programming languages. Your passion is software engineering and coming up with innovative solutions for customer problems. You will grow rapidly by driving high impact features to production with coaching from your veteran teammates.<br><br>This role will enable you to elevate your skills to the next level. As an intern, you will have regular 1:1s with your mentor, an experienced engineer on your team. You will get career advice through regular meetings with your manager, who is accountable for developing the careers of their reports. As part of your internship, you’ll get visibility by presenting your ideas, vision, and accomplishments to executive leadership.<br><br>Join Forces in an exciting environment where you use your passion, experience, and analytical skills to help bring new solutions to public safety and leave a positive impact on the world.</p>
<p><strong>What You'll Do</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Owning and driving end-to-end features with Product and Design</li>
<li>Working with the latest open source technologies</li>
<li>Participating in code reviews and architecture design meetings</li>
<li>Ensuring that your code hits a high bar for quality and performance</li>
<li>Daily involvement with all team activities, from standup and sprint planning to team morale events</li>
<li>Opportunity for on-site agency visits, including ride alongs</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What You Bring</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Pursuing graduation in a Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field at a community college, university, or bootcamp</li>
<li>Experience in at least one object-oriented language such as Java, Python, Scala, Go, C#, or similar</li>
<li>Effective communication and collaboration skills</li>
<li>Cultural alignment to Axon Company Values:<span class="Apple-converted-space">&nbsp;</span><a href="https://global.axon.com/company/values">https://global.axon.com/company/values</a></li>
<li>A previous software development internship</li>
<li>Up-to-date knowledge of the latest in software engineering and open source technologies</li>
<li>Good Linux/Unix/Mac experience</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Please Note</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Strive for a full 12 weeks</li>
<li>Semester interns: 24 May 2027 – 16 August 2027</li>
<li>Quarter interns: 7 June 2027 – 27 August 2027</li>
</ul>
