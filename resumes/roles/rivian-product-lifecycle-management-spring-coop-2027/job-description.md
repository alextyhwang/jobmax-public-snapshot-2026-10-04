No Visa Sponsorship Accelerated Vehicle Design: Automating Catia V6 regression testing allows Rivian to significantly speed up vehicle engineering cycles by instantly catching CAD integration errors that would otherwise require tedious manual inspection. Intelligent Quality Control: Integrating AI into this automation framework enables the system to predict and prioritize high-risk design changes, ensuring the structural and functional integrity of complex EV models after every system update. Strategic Talent Pipeline: Creating a dedicated Coop role for this project provides a cost-effective way to modernize Rivian's product lifecycle management (PLM) workflows while training early-career engineers in their specific software ecosystem. Responsibilities C++ API Integration: Programmatically interact with the Dassault Systèmes ecosystem by leveraging Catia V6 C++ APIs (CAA RADE) to extract geometric data, manipulate 3D assemblies, and execute automated sequences directly within the CAD environment. Agentic AI Implementation: Design and deploy autonomous AI agents capable of exploring complex vehicle assemblies, dynamically generating edge-case test scenarios, and prioritizing high-risk structural changes without human intervention. Full-Stack Application Development: Build and maintain scalable backend infrastructure and internal tooling that orchestrates the execution of these AI-driven tests, providing a seamless interface for Rivian's mechanical and software engineering teams. Automated Regression Architecture: Architect a robust continuous integration (CI) pipeline that connects the Catia V6 environment with the AI testing framework, ensuring comprehensive regression suites run reliably after every major PLM or vehicle design update. Intelligent Debugging & Analysis: Analyze complex geometric failures and systemic anomalies flagged by the AI agents, collaborating cross-functionally to resolve bugs, refine the testing algorithms, and ensure the structural integrity of Rivian's EV platforms. Qualifications Must be currently pursuing a bachelors or masters degree at an accredited university. Actively pursuing a degree or one closely related in computer science or a closely related computational field. C++ & Spatial Geometry: Strong proficiency in C++ object-oriented programming with a solid grasp of 3D mathematics, vector geometry, and ideally, prior exposure to complex CAD APIs like Catia V6 CAA RADE. Applied Agentic AI: Demonstrated experience or advanced coursework working with autonomous AI agents, large language models (LLMs), or reinforcement learning frameworks applied to automated decision-making and workflow orchestration. Modern Software Engineering: Hands-on experience building scalable backend services or full-stack applications using Python or similar modern languages, coupled with working knowledge of REST APIs, version control (Git), and CI/CD pipelines. Cross-Disciplinary Execution: Exceptional analytical problem-solving skills with the ability to translate mechanical engineering constraints into robust software architecture, driven by a strong passion for the electric vehicle industry.
