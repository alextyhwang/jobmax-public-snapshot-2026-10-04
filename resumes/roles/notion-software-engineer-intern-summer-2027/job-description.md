## Full Job Description

Who We Are

Notion is the collaborative AI workspace where teams and agents think together. It brings knowledge, projects, meetings, and AI tools into one place for millions of individuals, teams, and companies. Notion is building systems of record, AI agents, and automation for work.

Internship Dates and Location

- May 17-August 6, 2027, or June 14-September 3, 2027, based on the student's summer schedule.
- Interns must work from Notion's San Francisco or New York office during the internship.

What You'll Achieve

- Write clean, secure, tested, and documented code.
- Design and enhance the Notion product with new capabilities and an AI-first mindset.
- Develop, fix, and debug software across the full stack, including web services, databases, applications, and tools, using modern frameworks and tooling.

Qualifications

- Pursuing a bachelor's or master's degree in computer science, engineering, or another related field; must graduate before Summer 2028.
- Available for one of the listed internship date ranges and able to work from the New York or San Francisco office.
- Previous internship experience.
- Working toward proficiency in one or more languages such as TypeScript, Node.js, or Python.
- Excited by large challenges and by discovering problems as well as solving them.
- Able to problem-solve and adapt to changing priorities in a fast-paced environment.

Skills You'll Need to Bring

- Thoughtful problem-solving grounded in accurate context, decomposition, clean solutions, and asking for help when needed.
- A user-first approach and awareness of the real-world impact of the product.
- Pragmatic technology judgment based on tradeoffs and willingness to learn.
- Clear, empathetic written and real-time communication.
- Cross-functional teamwork, learning, and helping teammates grow.
- AI enthusiasm demonstrated through building or prototyping with LLMs, embeddings, or machine learning.

Nice to Haves

- Expertise with TypeScript, React, or Python.
- Familiarity with computing pioneers such as Ada Lovelace, Douglas Engelbart, and Alan Kay.
- Interests outside technology, including art, history, or social sciences.

Compensation and AI Note

- Estimated hourly pay is $57 for bachelor's students and $61 for master's students in San Francisco or New York City.
- Notion expects intellectual curiosity and thoughtful use of AI as a collaborator; AI fluency is explicit for roles where it is core.
