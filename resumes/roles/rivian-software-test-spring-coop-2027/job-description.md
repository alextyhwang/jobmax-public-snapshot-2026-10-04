As a Software Test Engineer Intern at Rivian, you'll have the exciting opportunity to work on real-world challenges in the rapidly evolving field of electric vehicle charging. You will be developing innovative test strategies, automating test procedures, and analyzing results to ensure our charging systems exceed expectations. By ensuring our charging systems are robust and reliable, you'll directly impact the quality of Rivian's charging products and contribute to the future of sustainable transportation! Responsibilities Develop cutting-edge automated test suites and frameworks to rigorously validate the performance of Rivian's charging systems, pushing the boundaries of efficiency and reliability. Design and implement innovative test automation frameworks and scripts, ensuring seamless integration with our CI/CD pipeline for rapid iteration and deployment of software updates. Collaborate closely with software development teams to integrate automated tests into the development process, enabling early detection of issues and faster delivery of high-quality charging solutions. Develop and maintain comprehensive Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) testing frameworks to simulate real-world scenarios and stress conditions, ensuring the robustness and safety of Rivian's charging infrastructure. Enhance and optimize existing HIL platforms, implementing upgrades and modifications to maximize performance, efficiency, and scalability, pushing the limits of our testing capabilities. Qualifications Actively pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in at an accredited university. Majoring in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field. Excellent Python programming skills with an emphasis on test automation. Knowledge of OOP concepts is a plus. Hands-on experience with test automation frameworks, such as pytest. Ability to analyze software requirements and design comprehensive test cases that validate functionality and performance.
