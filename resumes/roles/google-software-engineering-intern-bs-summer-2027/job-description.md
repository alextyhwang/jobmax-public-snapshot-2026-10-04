Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. The anticipated application window is open until September 25, 2026, but may close earlier if all projects are full. The internship requires candidates to be located in the United States for the duration. The role is intended for students in all academic years pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field. English proficiency is required.

Applicants need an updated resume and a current unofficial or official transcript in English. The internship is a 12-14 week paid program. Software Engineering and Site Reliability Engineering placements are available across North America, with recruitment determining the best fit.

## Minimum qualifications

- Pursuing a Bachelor's degree or post-secondary training experience focused on software development or another technical field.
- Experience in software development and coding in two or more general-purpose programming languages such as C, C++, Java, JavaScript, or Python.
- Experience with data structures or algorithms from school, work, or other settings.

## Preferred qualifications

- Currently attending a degree program in the United States and available to work full time for 12 weeks outside the university term.
- Returning to a degree program after the internship.
- Experience programming in three or more of C, C++, C#, Java, JavaScript, Go, Python, or similar languages.
- Experience with AI/ML, infrastructure, web application development, Unix/Linux environments, mobile application development, distributed and parallel systems, machine learning, information retrieval, natural language processing, networking, large software systems, or security software development.

## About the job

The Software Engineering Internship program offers work on complex computer science solutions, scalable distributed software systems, and smaller projects with broad appeal. Google is advancing computing and building intelligent systems across its products through current computer science techniques, algorithms, and AI systems.

Software Engineering interns work on projects critical to Google's needs as versatile engineers addressing new problems. They design, test, deploy, and maintain software solutions.

Site Reliability Engineering interns create, fix, extend, and scale code; improve reliability and performance at massive scale; troubleshoot traffic anomalies; maintain services; build monitoring and alerts; and develop automation infrastructure.

## Responsibilities

- Create and support a team while working with peers, managers, and other teams.
- Develop scripts to automate routine tasks.
- Analyze information and evaluate results to choose effective solutions.
- Apply computer science coursework to real-world challenges.
