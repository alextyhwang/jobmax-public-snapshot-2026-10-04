## Role

Build data pipelines and machine-learning workflows in Python for real-world products. Develop, train, and deploy models with frameworks such as PyTorch or scikit-learn, and orchestrate large-scale data solutions with tools such as Spark and Kubernetes. Build and scale intelligent data pipelines and distributed systems so AI platforms remain performant and reliable while processing data at scale.

## Minimum qualifications

- Currently enrolled in an undergraduate associate's or bachelor's program in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Applied Mathematics, or a related field.
- Expected to continue enrollment after the internship.
- Python and core data structures demonstrated through an academic, personal, or professional project.
- Exposure to TensorFlow, PyTorch, or scikit-learn.
- Working knowledge of Spark, Hadoop/MapReduce, or Flink.
- Hands-on experience with Docker or Kubernetes.

## Preferred qualifications

- Experience deploying or productionizing a trained ML model beyond coursework-level training and evaluation.
- Exposure to AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
- Understanding of at least two of scalability, reliability, fault tolerance, or data consistency.
- Working knowledge of at least two of load balancing, consensus algorithms, or inter-service communication.
