# Research A Company Playbook

Use this playbook when asked to populate or improve a company outreach file.

## Goal

Find credible people to contact for coffee chats, recruiting conversations, referrals, and team-specific learning.

## Research Targets

| Target Type | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| University recruiters | Best path for internship and co-op process clarity |
| SWE internship recruiters | Direct alignment with the Spring 2027 and Summer 2027 opportunity search |
| Engineers on AI product, agent tooling, applied LLM, developer tools, or deployed/product teams | Best path for team insight, referrals, and realistic role fit |
| RIT alumni | Strongest natural warm connection |
| Bay Area connections | Useful geographic and personal overlap |
| Founders or early engineers | Useful for unicorns and smaller AI companies |

## Process

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open the target company file in `outreach/` |
| 2 | Read the company snapshot and current `Contact CRM` |
| 3 | Search for recruiters, engineers, alumni, and team-specific contacts |
| 4 | Verify each person's company, role, and public profile before adding |
| 5 | Add rows with `Researching` or `Ready` status |
| 6 | Fill `Source URL`, `LinkedIn`, and `Notes` whenever available |
| 7 | Avoid storing private emails unless they are public or manually provided |

## Quality Bar

- A good row has a name, current role, public source URL, likely relevance, and a specific next action.
- Do not add large lists of low-confidence people.
- Prioritize excellent, well-verified contacts and do not impose a fixed count.
- Keep notes factual and useful for future message drafting.
