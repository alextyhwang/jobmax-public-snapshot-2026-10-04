# Photos For BASA x SanDisk Personal Leadership Post

Drag the final personal post photos into this folder.

Recommended set: 4-6 photos.

Preferred order:

1. the candidate speaking, presenting, or leading.
2. Students working or listening.
3. Robotics challenge or hackathon activity.
4. the candidate with BASA team, volunteers, or event context.
5. SanDisk event or venue context.
6. Wide room shot showing scale.

Privacy check:

- The first image should clearly show the candidate leading.
- Avoid close-up identifiable student photos unless BASA has permission.
- Avoid exposing student names, badges, emails, sign-in sheets, or private materials.
